Privacy Policy

Veilux is built around privacy-first infrastructure. This page explains what information is collected through the website, how operational records are used, and how communication preferences are managed.

Data We Receive
  • Contact and newsletter details that you voluntarily provide.
  • Basic device, browser, and page analytics used to improve site performance.
  • Product interaction records stored locally in the browser for demo and workspace continuity.
How We Use It
  • Respond to partnership, product, and support inquiries.
  • Share Veilux product updates, launch notes, and developer information when requested.
  • Maintain service quality, detect abuse, and improve UX across site surfaces.

Privacy Commitments

Veilux does not sell contact information. Operational data is limited to what is required for communications, product support, and service improvement. Sensitive app behavior shown in frontend product experiences is stored locally unless and until production APIs are introduced.

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@veilux.network.
